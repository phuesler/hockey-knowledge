Many of you have a device with wifi only, such as an iPod or an iPad. There is no wifi on
the bus to a game. So that you can still look up how a face-off works or where you stand
in defence, this site saves itself on your device.

<Callout type="merke" title="In short">
1. Open the site **on wifi**.
2. **Add it to your home screen**.
3. Tap the new icon **once while still on wifi**.

From then on the site works **without internet** too.
</Callout>

## How it works

On your first visit the browser downloads every article in the background. That is about
1.5 megabytes, less than a single phone photo. Without internet the browser shows the
saved copy.

When you are online, the site fetches the latest version automatically. New articles
arrive by themselves as soon as you open the site on wifi.

## iPhone, iPad and iPod

On Apple devices the **home screen** step matters. Safari deletes saved sites that you
have not opened for seven days. Sites on the home screen are not deleted.

1. Open the site in **Safari** while you are on wifi.
2. Tap the **share icon**. It is the square with the arrow pointing up.
3. Choose **"Add to Home Screen"** (Zum Home-Bildschirm) and then **"Add"** (Hinzufügen).
4. The FASS icon appears on your home screen. **Tap it once** while you are still on wifi.

The last step is not a mistake. The icon on the home screen has its own storage, separate
from Safari. So it has to download the articles once by itself.

## Android

1. Open the site in **Chrome** while you are on wifi.
2. Tap the **three dots** in the top right corner.
3. Choose **"Add to Home screen"** (Zum Startbildschirm hinzufügen) or **"Install app"**
   (App installieren).

On Android the site works offline even without this step. With the icon you just find it
faster on the bus.

## Try it out

At home, switch on **flight mode** and open the site from the icon. Click through a few
articles. If that works, it works on the bus too.

<Callout type="tipp" title="Before match day">
Open the site once on wifi the evening before the game. Then you are sure to have the
latest version with you.
</Callout>

## What does not work offline

Links to other sites, such as the club website or GitHub, still need internet. Everything
on this site itself can be read offline.

<Callout type="merke" title="Slightly different on every device">
The names of the menu items depend on the device and its version. The steps above are a
guide. If something does not work, ask us at training.
</Callout>
